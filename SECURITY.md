# Security policy

Please don't report security vulnerabilities in public issues.

Use GitHub's private reporting instead: open the affected repository, go to **Security → Report a vulnerability**, and describe the issue with steps to reproduce. If the problem is in the Crossly service itself rather than in one of these repositories, report it against [crossly-sdk](https://github.com/crossly-dev/crossly-sdk/security/advisories/new).

We acknowledge reports within three business days and keep you updated until it's fixed. Please give us a reasonable window to ship a fix before disclosing publicly.

Out of scope: rate limiting and denial of service, scanner output without a demonstrated impact, and issues in the third-party marketplaces themselves.
